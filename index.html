What to limit and what to keep

Limit or avoid

  • Sugary drinks, fruit juice, sweet chai and coffee (swap for water, buttermilk, spearmint or green tea)
  • Maida, bakery items, biscuits and packaged snacks
  • Deep-fried food and street snacks
  • Large portions of white rice or plain potato
  • Crash diets, skipped meals and long fasts (raise stress hormones)
  • Daily HIIT or workouts on little sleep
  • Late-night heavy meals and screens before bed
  • Smoking and regular alcohol
  • Starting supplements or medication without your doctor

Keep doing

  • Protein in every meal and snack
  • Vegetables and protein first, carbs last
  • A 10-15 minute walk after meals
  • Jowar, bajra, ragi, oats, millets and whole wheat
  • Nuts, seeds, flax and fatty fish for healthy fats
  • Strength training 3 days a week
  • A fixed sleep and wake time
  • 10 minutes a day to wind down: breathing, a walk or journaling
  • Tracking waist, cycle regularity and energy, not just the scale

Ask your doctor about

Fasting glucose and insulin (or HbA1c), lipid profile, thyroid (TSH), prolactin, vitamin D, B12 and hormone levels. Based on results, a doctor may suggest metformin, myo-inositol or vitamin D.

These are general estimates, not medical advice. Your needs vary with age, activity and test results, so check the plan with your gynecologist or a registered dietitian. If you lose weight faster than 0.5 kg a week, eat a bit more.
